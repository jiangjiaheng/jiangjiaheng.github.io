# Daily English Sentences

  1. It’s pretty easy to fail. Use pretty instead of so
  2. 可以的：it is ok
  3. 没问题：no problem
  4. 我将要做什么：I’m going to do sth , instead of will
  5. 等一下：hold on
  6. 你什么时候有空：when are you free to do sth (when are you free to visit me?)
  7. 我想你了，想和你聊聊天：I miss you and want to talk with you
  8. 你愿意吗：would you like to do sth or I would like to do sth
  9. 还行：not too bad ; My health is not too bad
  10. 纸上谈兵：you talk about this stuff like you read it in a book
  11. 我宁愿相信你而不是其他人：I would rather believe you than other
  12. 好吧，告诉我：Well, tell me
  13. 告诉我他们是什么：Tell me what they are
  14. 你觉得如何，你什么意思；What do you mean or think
  15. 主动被动：The police dog set on this criminal or this criminal was set on by the police dog
  16. 主语在前定语在后，动作在前副词在后，状语最后，问句疑问词在前
  17. she comes upon me in coffee shop
  18. I am still on good terms with she
  19. 要考：it will be examined
  20. 对什么有利：the score is 10 to 4 in our favour
  21. 我正要说这个：I am getting to that
  22. 这个是什么: what about this or sth
  23. 发传单：I’m handing these papers out
  24. 首先: first and foremost
  25. 这个男人接近哭泣：the man was bordering on crying
  26. 差不多，举例来说：yeah, more or less,the us has a market capitalist economy, for instance
  27. 社会主义与资本主义：the socialism and the capitalism
  28. 中央计划经济与市场经济: the centrally planned economy and the market economy
  29. 哪个先: which first
  30. 指的是: respiration refers to the process of inhaling and exhaling air
  31. 搞混了：I am already confused
  32. 确实是：exactly
  33. 我再仔细听: I am listening very carefully
  34. 对了：you got it
  35. 这个可能有点不好理解，但是我会解释：it’s not that hard to understand. Well,maybe a little,but I’ll try to explain
  36. 这就是个障眼法：it’s just an eye trick
  37. 报仇：the cat paid the dog back for dirting her clothes on purpose
  38. 回忆: the dumplings brought happy childhood memories to mind
  39. 告诉我：really, tell me about it
  40. 我发誓这是真的：I swear I’m tell the truth
  41. 他昨晚去世了：he passed away in his sleep last night
  42. 我完全同意：I totally agree
  43. 时不时回顾你学过的东西：go over what you learned from time to time
  44. 就是这样：that’s right
  45. 放飞自我：I really let myself go while on vacation
  46. 关你屁事：mind your own business
  47. 一开始：from the outside I knew you
  48. 一下子：the domino pieces fell at a stroke
  49. 把我说成坏人：the colleagues make me out to be a bad person
  50. 顺便说一下，我饿死了：by the way, I’m starving
  51. 这家店一直营业：the store is opening at all times
  52. 她正在镜子前化妆：the young lady is making herself up in front of the mirror
  53. 保持承诺：keep to one’s promise
  54. 成交：we have a deal
  55. 我手上有500元: I have 500yuan on hand
  56. 收工：she calls it a day and goes home
  57. 有潜质：the boy have the makings of a great soccer player
  58. 我看错了：I misread
  59. 我错了：it's my fault
